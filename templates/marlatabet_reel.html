How these come together for you

Each clip above is a single fragment, three to six seconds long, one camera move, one mood. On its own it is reference. Cut together with intent, the fragments become an ad.

For your line we would render eight to twelve fragments per concept. A hero close up of the stone, a worn shot on a model, a packaging detail, a hand near the face, a mood frame. We then edit those into a 15 or 30 second ad, score it, and finish with your brand language on the end card.

The result reads as a $150,000 production. In every frame outside of the rendering itself, it actually is one.
