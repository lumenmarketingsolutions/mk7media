The story

Two people. One vision.

MK7 started the way the best things do. With a vision nobody else could see yet, and two people who refused to wait for permission.

Mary saw it first. She knew what Lebanese businesses could become, and she believed they deserved marketing as good as anything in the world.

Kendall was running an agency in America and saw the same gap from the other side. The help available in Lebanon was nowhere near the standard he knew. So he picked up his desk and moved it to Beirut.

Together they built MK7 on the skills they spent their lives learning, and on one simple idea. Help other people grow their dreams the same way we grow ours.

Today MK7 is a team of marketers, creators and developers in Beirut, working with brands across nine countries.
