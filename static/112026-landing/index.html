Your questions, answered

How much does treatment cost?

The full treatment is $2,300. That covers your 3D smile design, every aligner in your plan, your retainers, your first refinement, the tracking app, and complimentary whitening.

If you prefer, you can start with a $500 deposit for your smile design and pay the balance when your aligners are ready.

How long does treatment take?

It depends on how much your teeth need to move. You will see your exact timeline in your 3D smile design at the first visit, before you commit to anything.

Am I a candidate for clear aligners?

Aligners treat crowded teeth, gaps, overbites, underbites and crossbites, for patients aged 14 and above. The doctor confirms it at your first visit after your scan. If aligners are not right for you, we will tell you honestly.

Is my result guaranteed?

When you wear your aligners as planned and log them in the Fixaligner app, your first refinement is included at no extra cost if your teeth need a final adjustment.

Does it hurt?

There are no wires or brackets. Most people feel light pressure for the first day or two of each new aligner, which is a sign that your teeth are moving.

What happens at the first visit?

It takes about 30 minutes. We take a 3D scan and photos of your teeth, and the doctor checks that aligners are right for you. Please bring a panoramic X ray. The visit is complimentary.

I live abroad. Can I still do this?

Yes. You can start during a visit to Beirut. Your aligners are ready the next day, and the app lets you follow your treatment from wherever you live.
